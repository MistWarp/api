# Record of processing activities (processor)

Kept under UK GDPR Article 30(2). Version 2026-10-01.

**Processor:** Sophie, sole trader trading as Rotur and MistWarp, United Kingdom. MistWarp is part of Rotur. Contact: privacy@mistwarp.org. MistWarp has no representative or data protection officer; it is not required to appoint one.

**Controllers:** every school, trust, district or organisation whose teacher has accepted the Classroom Terms. The organisation's name, the accepting teacher, the terms version and the time are stored in the `classroom_terms` collection, with the history of every acceptance.

**Categories of processing carried out for each controller:**

- Hosting student accounts and their sign-in.
- Storing and displaying projects, assignments, submissions, feedback, grades and groups.
- Live group editing and class presentations.
- Moving a student's projects to a personal account on the controller's instruction.
- Producing data downloads for the controller.
- Deleting data on instruction and under the retention schedule.

**Transfers outside the UK:** project files and traffic through Cloudflare, Inc. (United States and its global network), covered by Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

**Security measures:** see [security-programme.md](security-programme.md).
